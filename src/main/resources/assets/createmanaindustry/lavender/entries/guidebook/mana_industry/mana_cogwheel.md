A Mana Cogwheel obtains rotation from a mist field. Supply a Kinetic Atomizer with [Liquid Mana](^createmanaindustry:mana_industry/liquid_mana) and place the cogwheel within its field to obtain a speed of **256**.

Liquid Media mist gives a speed of **−256** instead. The two fluids drive the wheel in opposite directions.

;;;;;

<|page-title@lavender:book_components|title=Maintaining the Field|>

Its stress capacity is **8 × absolute speed SU**, providing 2,048 SU at these speeds. Outside an effective mist field, it no longer provides rotation.

The atomizer needs fluid and rotational power to maintain its field. Arrange a continuous supply before relying on the cogwheel to drive a production line.
