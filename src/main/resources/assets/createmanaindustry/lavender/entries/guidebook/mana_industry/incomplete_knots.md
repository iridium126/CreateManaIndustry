[Knots](^trickster:items/mana/knots) need not be made one spell at a time. A Deployer holding a Glass block can begin their production from materials on a belt.

Amethyst Shards and Quartz become finished Knots immediately. Emeralds, Prismarine Crystals, Diamonds, Echo Shards and Nether Stars first become **Incomplete Knots**.

;;;;;

<|page-title@lavender:book_components|title=Supplying Mana|>

Fill an Incomplete Knot with [Liquid Mana](^createmanaindustry:mana_industry/liquid_mana). A Spout processes at most one bucket per operation. The blue progress bar records the mana already invested in its creation.

Once the creation cost is met, the item becomes a complete Knot immediately. **No pressing step is required to finish it.**

;;;;;

<|page-title@lavender:book_components|title=Creation Costs|>

The default creation costs are:

- Emerald Knot: 512 G
- Prismatic Knot: 8,192 G
- Diamond Knot: 8,192 G
- Echo Knot: 65,536 G
- Astral Knot: 524,288 G

These are creation costs, rather than storage capacities.

;;;;;

<|page-title@lavender:book_components|title=Cracking the Result|>

If a [Cracked Knot](^trickster:items/mana/cracked_knots) is wanted, pass the finished Knot through a Mechanical Press. This optional step produces the corresponding cracked variant.

Collect complete Knots before the press, and cracked ones after it.
